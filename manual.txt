1.Safety Information
Before installing and using the Intelligent Bionic Robotic Hand, please carefully read
the information in this chapter. Below are the essential safety usage details for the
dexterous hand. This information is intended to protect you, the driver, and related
equipment when using the product.

Cautions
Reminder:
• Disconnect the power supply before installation, cleaning, or maintenance.
• Always operate the product within the specified range of parameters.
• Do not use damaged power cables, plugs, or loose sockets, as this may damage the
product.
• Avoid touching electrical components to prevent damage from electrostatic
discharge.
• Ensure the end effector is correctly and securely mounted and connected via the
cable.
• The Intelligent Bionic Robotic hand's power supply current is limited to 2A-3A to
prevent excessive current during stalling, which could damage the equipment.

2. Product Introduction
2.1 Product Overview
The Intelligent Bionic Robotic Hand is a humanoid robotic hand integrated with 6
actuators, allowing each finger to move independently. It is highly flexible and
adaptable, capable of interacting with the surrounding environment, objects, and even
humans, while minimizing the risks of injuring the operator, damaging the product, or
harming the robot itself. The communication methods used include RS485, Can, and
Bluetooth interfaces.

3. Communication Protocol
3.1Overview of Communication Protocol
RS-485 Communication Protocol
RS-485 (also known as EIA-485) is an industrial standard serial communication
protocol used to connect multiple devices. It is a half-duplex communication protocol
that allows data transmission between multiple devices, with a maximum of 32
devices connected. One device acts as the master, while the other devices function as
slaves.
The advantages of RS-485 technology include its ability to transmit data over
long distances, with a maximum distance of 1200 meters. It also supports high
transmission speeds, reaching up to 10 Mbps. Additionally, RS-485 is highly resistant
to interference, making it capable of withstanding noise and operating in
environments with electromagnetic interference (EMI).
RS-485 technology is widely used in various applications, primarily in industrial
control systems such as robotic control systems, automation control systems, and
smart home systems.
In summary, RS-485 is a highly useful serial communication protocol. Its long
transmission distance, high data transfer rate, and strong resistance to interference
make it suitable for a range of applications, including industrial control systems, data
acquisition systems, intelligent transportation systems, and many other scenarios.
CAN Communication Protocol
The main features of the CAN bus are as follows:
High Reliability: The CAN bus uses differential signaling for data transmission,
which provides strong resistance to interference. It also features error detection and
recovery mechanisms, significantly enhancing the system's reliability.
Real-Time Capability: The CAN bus utilizes bit arbitration technology, ensuring
that high-priority messages are transmitted in a timely manner. This makes it ideal for
applications that require real-time responses.
Flexibility: The CAN bus supports various communication speeds, with the
highest speed reaching up to 1 Mbps. It also supports a range of data lengths, from 8
bits to 8192 bits, allowing for flexible configuration based on specific needs.
International Standard: The CAN bus is an international standard and is widely used
worldwide, facilitating technical exchanges and product interoperability.
